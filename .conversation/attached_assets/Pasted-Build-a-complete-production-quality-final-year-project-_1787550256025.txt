Build a complete, production-quality final-year project called:

# CrowdSentinel

### AI-Based Early Crowd Risk and Stampede Detection System

You are acting as a **senior AI engineer, computer vision engineer, full-stack developer, UI/UX designer, and software architect**.

Do not create a simple demo or static dashboard.

Build a **fully functional, modular, realistic computer-vision-based crowd monitoring application** that can be demonstrated during a final-year project review.

> Analyze CCTV/video footage, detect people, estimate crowd density, analyze crowd movement, identify abnormal crowd conditions, calculate a crowd-risk level, and generate an early warning.

The system must NOT claim that it can perfectly predict a stampede.
It should identify **potentially dangerous crowd conditions and increasing risk** based on measurable visual indicators.
