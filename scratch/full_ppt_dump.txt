  3. Problem Statement
--- TextBox 3 ---
  Large crowds in places such as stadiums, railway stations, concerts, and public gatherings can become dangerous when crowd density increases or people suddenly change their movement. Existing CCTV monitoring mainly depends on human observation, which makes it difficult to continuously monitor large crowds and identify risky situations at an early stage. Therefore, there is a need for an automated system that can analyze crowd density and movement from video footage, detect abnormal crowd behavior, and provide an early warning when the situation becomes potentially dangerous. However, challenges such as people overlapping each other, different camera angles, poor lighting, and false alarms need to be considered.

  4
============================== SLIDE 8 ==============================
--- Rectangle 1 ---
--- TextBox 2 ---
  4. Existing System
--- TextBox 3 ---
  Currently, crowd monitoring is mainly carried out using CCTV cameras and manual observation, where security personnel watch the crowd and identify unusual situations. Some systems also use basic image processing, people counting, and crowd density estimation to understand the number of people in an area. However, these approaches mainly focus on monitoring or counting the crowd rather than analyzing its behavior. Continuous manual monitoring is difficult, especially when multiple cameras are involved, and existing methods may struggle with high crowd density, overlapping people, different camera angles, poor lighting, and sudden changes in crowd movement. As a result, dangerous situations may not be identified early enough for timely action.

--- Rectangle 1 ---
--- TextBox 2 ---
  5. Proposed System
--- TextBox 3 ---
  The proposed system uses Artificial Intelligence and Computer Vision to automatically monitor crowd video and identify potentially dangerous conditions. It detects people, analyzes crowd density and movement patterns, and checks for unusual changes in crowd flow. Based on these factors, the system estimates the level of risk and can generate an early warning when abnormal crowd conditions are detected. Compared with traditional manual monitoring, this approach provides continuous and automated analysis, helping authorities respond more quickly. The major features include person detection, crowd density analysis, movement analysis, abnormal behavior detection, risk assessment, and alert generation. The expected benefit is improved crowd monitoring, faster identification of risky situations, and better support for public safety.

  8. Research Gap
--- TextBox 3 ---
  What has already been done?
  Existing studies use AI and Computer Vision for crowd monitoring.
  Researchers have explored crowd counting, density estimation, and movement analysis.
  Deep learning and optical flow are also used for abnormal crowd detection.
  What limitations exist?
  Some methods focus only on crowd density or people counting.
  Others focus mainly on abnormal movement or stampede classification.
  Performance can be affected by occlusion, lighting, camera angle, and high crowd density.
  What is missing?
  A practical system that combines density and movement analysis for early risk identification.
  A simple risk assessment approach that can work with real-time CCTV/video.
  Better focus on early warning rather than detecting the event only after it occurs.
  How does our project address the gap?
  Combines person detection, crowd density, and movement analysis.
  Uses these factors to estimate the crowd risk level.
  Generates an early warning when potentially dangerous conditions are detected.
  Focuses on a practical and understandable approach for real-time crowd monitoring.

  11. Modules
--- TextBox 3 ---
  1. Video Input & PreprocessingCollects CCTV/live video or recorded footage and prepares frames for further analysis.
  2. Person Detection & TrackingDetects people in each frame and tracks their movement across consecutive frames.
  3. Crowd Density AnalysisAnalyzes the number and concentration of people to identify areas with high crowd density.
  4. Movement & Behavior AnalysisStudies crowd speed, direction, and flow to identify sudden or unusual movement patterns.
  5. Risk Assessment & Alert GenerationCombines crowd density and movement information to determine the crowd risk level and generate alerts for high-risk conditions.
  6. Monitoring & Data StorageDisplays the detected crowd condition and risk level through a dashboard while storing detection results, alerts, and analysis history.

  13. Dataset / Data Source
--- TextBox 3 ---
  Dataset Name & Source
  UMN Unusual Crowd Activity Dataset – public crowd abnormality dataset from the University of Minnesota.
  GBA-Stampedes & GSMADC – stampede/crowd-action datasets used in recent stampede-detection research.
  Number / Type of Data
  UMN contains 11 videos from 3 different scenes, with normal and abnormal crowd activity.
  GBA-Stampedes contains 12 videos, while GSMADC contains 37 sequences covering different crowd actions and environments.
  Important Features / Attributes
  Crowd density and number of people
  Movement speed and direction
  Normal vs abnormal crowd behavior
  Frame-level indication of abnormal activity/stampede onset where available
  Training / Testing Split
  Use separate videos/scenes for training and testing to reduce data leakage.
  A practical starting point is 70% training, 15% validation, and 15% testing, followed by evaluation on unseen videos.
  Preprocessing
  Extract frames from videos
  Resize frames to the model input size
  Normalize pixel values
  Remove corrupted/duplicate frames if necessary
  Generate labels for normal and abnormal/risk conditions

--- TextBox 3 ---
  AI / ML Algorithm
  Name of Algorithm / ModelYOLO (You Only Look Once) – for real-time person detection.
  Working PrincipleYOLO processes each video frame and identifies people by drawing bounding boxes around them. The detected people are then used for crowd density and movement analysis.
  Why It Was Selected
  Fast enough for real-time video processing.
  Performs well for detecting multiple people in a single frame.
  Easy to integrate with OpenCV and Python.
  Suitable for CCTV-based crowd monitoring.
  Expected Advantages
  Real-time person detection.
  Helps estimate crowd density.
  Provides input for tracking and movement analysis.
  Can support early identification of crowded or risky conditions.

  16. Performance Evaluation
--- TextBox 3 ---
  AI / ML Metrics
  Precision – Measures how many detected high-risk events are actually correct.
  Recall – Measures how many actual risky events are successfully detected.
  F1-Score – Gives a balanced measure of precision and recall.
  Accuracy – Measures the overall correctness of risk-level classification.
  False Alarm Rate – Measures how often the system incorrectly raises an alert.
  System / Software Metrics
  Response Time – Time taken from detecting a risky condition to generating an alert.
  Processing Speed (FPS) – Number of video frames processed per second.
  Reliability – Ability of the system to operate continuously without failure.
  Usability – How easily security personnel can understand and use the system.
